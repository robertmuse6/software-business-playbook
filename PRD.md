# From first use to a business

## Outcome
A mobile-first visual learning website that turns Alex Hormozi's public advice and software-business evidence into decisions for an early coaching/software venture.

## Underlying need
Connect distribution, pricing, user value, retention and team design. Make it possible to choose and test the next business assumption rather than consume another motivational summary.

## Recovered design brief
The user's September instructions: one linear A-to-Z concept spine; title each card with its takeaway; short Inshorts-like reading bursts with tap-for-depth; preserve depth in notes; show why one concept leads to the next; concrete if-this-then-that rules; explain in plain language; compare alternatives, including why they lose. These are paraphrases of the user's own requests, not a transcript export.

## Current scope overrides
The October 27 follow-up supplies the central journey: free offer → passive users → active users → paid users → core recurring users. Pricing and distribution belong at each transition. A signup is not activation. Product direction, audience, price, activation and recurring-use cadence remain hypotheses.

## Architecture
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, no analytics, no external tracking, no paid services. Tabs: lifecycle cards; software rails; sales and team; working lab; sources; recovered brief. Search/filter controls, progressive disclosure, scenario calculator and an editable experiment plan. Working-lab edits stay in local storage on this browser only; JSON export/import is provided. Nothing is sent to a coach, server or sales service.

## Privacy
Public learning content uses generic coaching and SaaS examples. No interview-level details, private financial anecdotes, customer identities, private documents or credentials. Product-specific material is used privately to guide relevance, not copied into the public repository.

## Evidence
Separate creator advice, software guidance, historical examples, and original applied hypotheses. Attach source IDs to cards and a source coverage ledger. Do not claim complete video transcription or audio verification. No fabricated success results, precise unsupported benchmarks, or invented timestamps. Lawful free sources only.

## Acceptance
Readable at 320,390,1280 pixels; no horizontal page overflow; all core content available without expansion; details open with keyboard; search no-result reset; tools calculate consistently; local-storage boundary visible; export/import work; all external citations point to observed sources; no changes to existing sites. Source files and full notes ship alongside the website.
